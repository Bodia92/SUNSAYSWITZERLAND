WHAT IS BESS AND WHO IS INVOLVED

A utility-scale battery energy storage system (BESS) draws power from the grid and releases it at peak demand — the backbone of a modern energy system. Every project brings together three main parties. SUNSAY NRG is the developer that connects them and carries the project from the first parcel of land to a ready-to-build state

Utility Scale BESS

  • Land Owner

    Provides land for projects under a long-term lease and receives stable rent payments with no operational involvement

  • Investor

    Provides project funding and receives a return on investment backed by a long-life infrastructure asset

  • Grid Operator

    Provides grid connection and gains a flexible asset that helps balance load and absorb renewable output peaks
