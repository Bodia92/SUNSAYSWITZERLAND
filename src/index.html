WHAT IS BESS AND WHO IS INVOLVED

A utility-scale battery energy storage system (BESS) draws power from the grid and releases it at peak demand — the backbone of a modern energy system. Every project brings together three main parties. SUNSAY NRG is the developer that connects them and carries the project from the first parcel of land to a ready-to-build state

Utility Scale BESS

  • Land Owner

    Provides land for projects under a long-term lease and receives stable rent payments with no operational involvement

  • Investor

    Provides project funding and receives a return on investment backed by a long-life infrastructure asset

  • Grid Operator

    Provides grid connection and gains a flexible asset that helps balance load and absorb renewable output peaks

How you participate

Two entry points: acquire a project to develop and build, or invest and hold for the long term

  • Acquire and Develop

    Development project acquisition
    Acquire a project at early-development stage (EDS) or ready-to-build (RTB) stage — partially or fully developed, depending on maturity. At ready-to-build, permits, grid connection agreements, and site rights are already secured, allowing you to take over at the construction stage without carrying permitting risk. At the early-development stage, you take over earlier, with the opportunity to shape the project to be fully fit for purpose.
    • Typical project size 5–50 MW
    • Development stage EDS or RTB certified
    • Handover Ownership, permits, contracts
    • Markets Switzerland, Austria

  • Invest and Hold

    Direct equity in the project
    Hold direct equity in the entity that owns a specific project — physical, inflation-protected infrastructure that generates revenue on regulated energy markets. We develop, deliver and, where required, oversee the asset on your behalf.
    • Target IRR from 7%
    • Payback from 4 years
    • Investment horizon 15+ years
    • Minimum ticket From 2M CHF/EUR
