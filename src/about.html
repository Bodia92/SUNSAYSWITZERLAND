Renewable energy developer

WHO WE ARE

SUNSAY NRG is a renewable-energy developer of utility-scale solar PV and battery storage projects

Headquartered in Switzerland, we operate across European markets, adapting each project to local energy systems, regulatory frameworks and pricing conditions

We take a project from the first parcel of land through grid connection and permitting to Ready-to-Build, then supervise trusted partners through construction and operations. You own the asset throughout.

Our Vision

We envision a future 100% powered by clean, renewable and affordable energy - available at any time, in any weather

Our mission

We develop key infrastructure for a stable energy system that gives everyone access to green energy
