SUNSAY NRG Suisse AG develops utility-scale solar PV and battery energy storage projects in Switzerland, Austria and Ukraine. We take projects from land origination through grid connection and permitting to Ready-to-Build, then supervise trusted partners through construction and operations while you own the asset. Investment opportunities are available to private and institutional investors, and Ready-to-Build projects to developers and utility companies.
